Generated/display frames remain separate.

Exercise at minimum:
- representative traversal across streamed regions;
- dense population/combat;
- destruction/environment transitions;
- D03 VFX/audio bursts;
- camera motion/rendering profile;
- repeated restart/reload/lifecycle;
- clean/warm shader/material path where current D03 evidence leaves a gap.
