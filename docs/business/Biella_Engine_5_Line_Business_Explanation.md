# Biella Engine - Five-Line Business Explanation

Biella Engine is a central AI production system that turns a business goal into organized, executable work.

It can coordinate different AI models, tools, computers, files, and specialist workflows instead of making you manage them one by one.

Biella is designed to plan the work, run tasks in parallel, track progress, recover from failures, check results, and deliver finished outputs.

Several building blocks and operating patterns have already been tested with real AI calls, coding agents, source synchronization, verification, and foundation tests; the central engine is still being integrated.

The end goal is simple: you describe the result you want, and Biella manages the AI-powered production process needed to get there.
