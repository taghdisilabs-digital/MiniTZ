## 2. What must be taken from the reference

### Industrial / mechanical credibility

Preserve the sense that the identity belongs to a real engineered system:
- load-bearing construction;
- believable joints and bearings;
- mechanical connection rather than decorative overlap;
- visible relationship between independent parts;
- strong physical mass;
- precision-fit assembly;
- controlled, credible motion potential.

The logo should feel engineered, not merely styled.

### Premium metal

Preserve:
- machined / forged material character;
- brushed and polished metal contrast;
- darker gunmetal structural surfaces;
- controlled bevel highlights;
- premium surface finish;
- physically plausible reflections.

Avoid:
- plastic appearance;
- excessive scratches or post-apocalyptic wear;
- chrome-everywhere treatment;
- decorative metal that obscures geometry.

### Purple identity and energy

Preserve:
- dominant Biella violet as the primary brand accent;
- restrained purple anodized/coated metal treatment;
- localized purple emission for active/cinematic states;
- purple energy that follows mechanical connection paths.

Purple must read as controlled activation, not generic neon.

### Precision engineering

Preserve:
- clear upper/lower structural relationship;
- connecting rod as the functional central bridge;
- bearing/joint logic;
- intentional alignment;
- readable negative space;
- repeatable geometry suitable for vector, 3D and manufacturing use.

### Restrained futuristic styling

The reference is futuristic because the engineering is refined and activated, not because it is covered in HUD graphics.

Preserve:
- clean dark presentation;
- controlled glow;
- limited technical accents;
- disciplined effects;
- cinematic depth.

Avoid:
- generic holographic UI;
- code rain;
- gratuitous sci-fi panels;
- arcade HUD framing;
- excessive lens flare;
- full-object neon.

### International business quality

The identity must work beyond a cinematic reveal.

Preserve qualities suitable for:
- enterprise presentations;
- investor materials;
- business documents;
- software/product UI;
- AAA game production;
- signage and physical manufacturing.

The mark must remain credible when all cinematic FX are removed.

## 3. Geometry interpretation boundaries

Until the exact approved vector master exists:

- do not redesign the symbol;
- do not simplify the silhouette;
- do not mirror or rotate it;
- do not change the upper/lower body relationship;
- do not change bearing placement;
- do not replace the connecting rod with a decorative line;
- do not introduce new symmetry merely for convenience;
- do not infer dimensions that are not measurable from the exact source bytes;
- do not claim a traced approximation is authoritative.

Any vector reconstruction must be checked against the exact `PRIMARY_REFERENCE.png` at pixel level before approval.

## 4. Wordmark interpretation

Take from the reference:
- uppercase `BIELLA`;
- uppercase `ENGINE`;
- engineered, wide, premium letterform direction;
- polished silver/warm-white primary wordmark treatment;
- violet may be used for `ENGINE`;

- high legibility.

Do not bake decorative sparks, glow or texture into authoritative wordmark geometry.

## 6. Application lessons from the board

The reference demonstrates that one identity must survive multiple expressions:
- full primary logo;
- icon/mark;
- monochrome;
- in-game/UI;
- merchandise / physical application.

This supports the production principle:

**One master geometry. Many expressions.**

A derivative may change material, background, lighting, composition or scale, but must not become a different logo.

## 7. What must not be taken literally

The reference board is an art-direction and geometry authority, not a requirement to reproduce every presentation element.

Do not treat these as locked logo components:
- piston photography;
- presentation-panel layout;
- mockup backgrounds;
- individual lighting setups;
- merchandise staging;
- supporting decorative grid/layout;
- cinematic sparks or environmental effects.

Those are examples of application context.
