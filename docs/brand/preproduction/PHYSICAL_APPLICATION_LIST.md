# Biella Engine — Physical Application List

Status: APPROVED PRE-PRODUCTION CONTROL  
Version: 1.0  
Package revision target: 1.2.0  
Scope: physical, manufactured, apparel, packaging and equipment applications of the Biella Engine identity

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the required physical-use families for Biella Engine so the logo can move from digital presentation into real manufactured surfaces without creating alternate identities.

All physical applications must derive from the future authoritative:

`02_LOGO/MASTER_LOGO_GEOMETRY.svg`

Until that vector master is approved, no manufacturing file may become authoritative from a raster reference, screenshot, 3D beauty render or manually redrawn approximation.

## 2. Core rule

**One master geometry. Many manufacturing expressions.**

Physical production may change:
- material;
- depth;
- surface finish;
- manufacturing process;
- scale;
- mounting method;
- ink / thread / foil treatment.

It may not change:
- symbol geometry;
- connecting-rod relationship;
- wordmark spelling;
- locked proportions;
- approved clear-space rules;
- core brand identity.

## 4. Required logo expressions for manufacturing

The manufacturing set must include:
- symbol-only monochrome;
- horizontal lockup monochrome;
- stacked lockup monochrome;
- one-color black;
- one-color white;
- engraving-safe geometry;
- emboss/deboss-safe geometry;
- print-safe vector;
- manufacturing outline where a process requires outline-only geometry;
- material-neutral geometry without baked metallic gradients;
- no-FX version of every mark.

A physical vendor must never be given only:
- a JPG;
- a cinematic PNG;
- a screenshot;
- a 3D beauty render;
- a glowing active-state raster.

## 5. Material application direction

### Brushed stainless steel
Use for premium plaques, equipment plates, signage and hardware identification.

Recommended visual treatment:
- dark recess or black fill where contrast is needed;
- optional violet secondary insert only when production method supports durable color.

### Dark gunmetal / anodized black
Use for equipment chassis, plaques, premium product badges and machinery/workstation applications.

Preferred logo:
- polished or engraved silver mark;
- one-color recessed mark;
- controlled violet insert if physically achievable.

### Purple anodized / coated metal
Use selectively. It may reinforce the upper-body identity or accent detail, but should not turn every physical object into a bright purple novelty product.

### Acrylic
Use for illuminated signage, reception installations and event displays. Lighting must preserve an acceptable off-state.

### Paper / card / rigid packaging
Use polished silver or neutral white, Biella Violet, and deep navy / black. Foil and spot UV are controlled premium finishes.

## 6. One-color manufacturing rule

The identity must work in one color with:
- no gradients;
- no glow;
- no shadows;
- no texture;
- no transparency;
- no orange sparks;
- no cyan accent requirement.

A one-color conversion is not allowed to redraw the connecting rod or replace bearings with generic dots.

## 7. Engraving / machining rules

Manufacturing geometry must:
- use closed paths;
- avoid accidental self-intersections;
- avoid duplicate coincident contours;
- preserve holes / negative spaces intentionally;
- remain derived from the approved vector master;
- be scaled without non-uniform distortion;
- document whether geometry is centerline, outline, pocket or filled area when the vendor needs that distinction.

Exact minimum stroke / gap values must come from the approved construction sheet and the chosen manufacturing process. Do not invent one universal millimeter value across all materials and vendors.

## 8. Emboss / deboss rules

Embossing and debossing may simplify depth and surface treatment, but must preserve:
- silhouette;
- bearing locations;
- connecting-rod relationship;
- wordmark proportions.

Production may require corner softening, minimum relief depth or minimum cavity spacing. These are process constraints, not permission to create a different logo.

## 9. Apparel rules

Apparel variants may use:
- embroidery;
- woven patch;
- screen print;
- transfer;
- reflective ink;
- tone-on-tone.

Preferred behavior:
- symbol-only at small scale;
- lockup at larger scale;
- no cinematic FX;
- no tiny tagline when it cannot reproduce cleanly.

Embroidery simplification must be generated from the vector master and recorded as a manufacturing derivative.

## 10. Packaging rules

Required packaging-capable expressions:
- full-color dark-background lockup;
- one-color black;
- one-color white;
- foil-ready vector;
- spot-UV mask;
- emboss/deboss source;
- small product-label mark.

Packaging may use industrial photography or cinematic product renders as supporting art, but the logo itself remains controlled vector geometry.

## 11. Equipment marking rules

For workstation, render node, server, controller, case or other equipment marking:
- prioritize readability;
- keep the symbol distinct from vents, ports and fasteners;
- do not place the mark where mechanical seams visually break the connecting rod;
- use durable manufacturing methods appropriate to the environment;
- retain an accessible one-color version.

## 12. Physical proof requirement

Every new manufacturing process requires at least one process-specific proof before it is considered production-approved.

Proof may be:
- test coupon;
- material swatch;
- vendor digital proof where physical proof is not yet practical;
- embroidery stitch-out;
- print proof;
- laser / engraving sample;
- molded sample.

A screen mockup alone is not manufacturing acceptance.

## 13. Color reproduction

Physical color must be matched against the approved brand token system and process capabilities.

Rules:
- do not assume screen HEX values equal print, paint, anodizing, thread or foil values;
- record vendor/material color references in the production job;
- proof violet under actual substrate and lighting;
- preserve neutral one-color fallback when exact violet reproduction is unavailable.

## 14. Source and delivery

Physical production source belongs under:

`10_PHYSICAL/SOURCE/`

Manufacturing-ready derivatives belong under:

`10_PHYSICAL/EXPORTS/`

Final broadly reusable vector exports also appear under:

`11_EXPORTS/PRINT/`

A vendor-specific derivative does not become the logo master.

## 15. Acceptance

A physical application is acceptable only when:
- it derives from approved geometry;
- no placeholder or approximate mark is used;
- the manufacturing process has been proofed;
- the result remains recognizable without digital effects;
- the material / finish is consistent with the brand direction;
- the application has a reproducible source file;
- final vendor-ready files are listed in `EXPORT_MATRIX.csv`;
- production evidence can be traced to the source revision.
