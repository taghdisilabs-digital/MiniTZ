# Biella Engine — Brand Master Specification

Status: APPROVED PRE-PRODUCTION CONTROL  
Version: 1.0  
Authority: Founder-approved reference board supplied on 2026-08-27  
Canonical product name: **Biella Engine**

## 1. Brand Position

Biella Engine is a universal, project-neutral AI production and execution engine.

Its brand must communicate a specific idea:

> **Biella is the connecting force that turns independent capability into coordinated execution.**

The name is anchored in the mechanical meaning of *biella*: the connecting rod between piston and crankshaft. The brand is not a literal automotive brand. The connecting rod is the conceptual foundation for force transfer, coordination, motion, durability and engineered precision.

Biella Engine should feel credible in both:
- AAA game / real-time production environments;
- enterprise, technical and investor-facing contexts.

The identity must avoid generic “AI startup” symbolism.

## 2. Approved Tagline

**CONNECTING INTELLIGENCE TO EXECUTION**

The tagline is secondary to the company name and may be omitted at small sizes, but when present its wording must not be changed without founder approval.

## 3. Core Brand Narrative

The brand story is:

1. Independent systems can each be powerful.
2. Power without coordination is fragmented.
3. Biella connects those systems.
4. Biella transfers intent into controlled execution.
5. The result is useful, durable production motion.

Mechanical analogy:

- piston = a capability producing force;
- connecting rod / biella = the transfer and coordination mechanism;
- crankshaft = unified productive motion;
- engine = the whole system working continuously.

The logo therefore expresses a load-bearing connection between independent geometric bodies rather than depicting a chatbot, AI brain, neural network, robot, cloud or generic cube.

## 4. Approved Visual Direction

The approved reference establishes the following direction:

- realistic AAA-quality industrial engineering;
- premium machined metal;
- visible mechanical credibility;
- heavy, precise, load-bearing construction;
- cinematic dark environments;
- restrained purple energy;
- controlled orange heat/sparks in cinematic scenes only;
- strong contrast;
- premium product-render finish;
- believable material response;
- no toy-like sci-fi;
- no arcade-HUD look;
- no generic AI imagery.

The brand should feel like a real system that could exist physically.

## 5. Logo Concept

The approved symbol is constructed as a mechanical relationship:

- upper geometric body;
- lower geometric body;
- connecting rod;
- upper bearing/joint;
- lower bearing/joint;
- integrated negative space;
- engineered alignment between all parts.

The rod is not decoration. It is the semantic center of the identity.

The final `MASTER_LOGO_GEOMETRY.svg` must be a clean vector reconstruction of the approved reference geometry. Until that vector master exists, the supplied reference image is the geometry authority. Do not redesign, simplify, mirror, rotate, round, distort or reinterpret the symbol.

## 6. Wordmark

Primary wordmark:

**BIELLA**  
**ENGINE**

Direction:
- wide, engineered uppercase letterforms;
- premium industrial / motorsport-grade spacing;
- restrained, not aggressive;
- legible at large cinematic scale and small corporate scale;
- “BIELLA” normally in polished silver / warm white;
- “ENGINE” may use Biella violet.

The wordmark must remain clearly separate from decorative FX.

## 8. Metallic Treatment

Primary 3D material language:

- upper geometric body: purple anodized / coated metal;
- lower geometric body: dark machined gunmetal or steel;
- connecting rod: brushed / forged steel;
- bearings: polished steel with darker internal recess;
- bevels: physically plausible, sharp enough to feel engineered;
- edges: controlled highlights, never plastic glow;
- surface wear: minimal and premium; no post-apocalyptic damage;
- emission: localized purple activation, never full-object neon.

The default logo must also work without any metallic or lighting treatment.

## 9. Typography Direction

Typography must support two modes:

### Brand / cinematic
- wide uppercase;
- geometric / engineered;
- medium-to-bold weight;
- deliberate tracking;
- high legibility.

### Documentation / UI
- modern neutral sans-serif;
- compact;
- highly legible;
- suitable for dense professional software.

Exact commercial typeface selection is deferred to `TYPOGRAPHY_SPEC.md`. Until then, production must use replaceable fonts and must not embed an unlicensed typeface into the authoritative geometry.

## 10. Composition Rules

Approved logo arrangements:

1. symbol above wordmark;
2. symbol left of wordmark;
3. symbol alone;
4. monochrome symbol;
5. engraved / embossed physical mark;
6. active cinematic symbol with restrained purple emission.

The logo must remain recognizable with:
- no glow;
- no color;
- no texture;
- no background;
- one-color manufacturing output.

## 11. Motion Personality

Motion must feel:
- mechanical;
- weighted;
- purposeful;
- synchronized;
- high-torque;
- precise;
- fast only after alignment;
- physically coherent.

Do not use floating hologram assembly as the main motion language.

The approved intro logic is:
separate parts → movement → alignment → mechanical lock → energy fusion → activation → reveal.

## 12. What Must Never Change Without Founder Approval

The following are locked:

- company name: `Biella Engine`;
- approved tagline wording;
- the approved symbol concept;
- the mechanical connecting-rod meaning;
- upper/lower body relationship;
- connecting rod as the central bridge;
- bearing/joint logic;
- overall approved silhouette once `MASTER_LOGO_GEOMETRY.svg` is finalized;
- dominant violet + engineered metal identity;
- premium dark industrial presentation;
- requirement that the logo work as a clean vector and monochrome mark;
- requirement that the identity remain usable in AAA gaming, software, corporate and physical contexts.

## 13. Prohibited Drift

Do not turn the identity into:

- an AI brain;
- neural-network mesh;
- chatbot bubble;
- robot;
- cloud;
- generic hexagon technology logo;
- generic glowing cube;
- infinity symbol;
- cryptocurrency mark;
- esports mascot;
- arcade HUD;
- fantasy emblem;
- literal piston illustration as the standalone company logo;
- flame logo;
- lightning-bolt logo;
- excessive RGB / rainbow treatment;
- orange-and-blue game UI branding;
- overcomplicated mechanical assembly that fails at icon size.

## 14. Source-of-Truth Order

For brand production, authority is:

1. founder-approved current instruction;
2. `BRAND_MASTER_SPEC.md`;
3. `PRIMARY_REFERENCE.png`;
4. future `MASTER_LOGO_GEOMETRY.svg` once approved;
5. construction/component/material specifications;

## 15. Production Principle

**One master geometry. Many expressions.**

All 2D, 3D, motion, print, UI, cinematic and physical variants must derive from the same approved identity rather than becoming separate logos.
