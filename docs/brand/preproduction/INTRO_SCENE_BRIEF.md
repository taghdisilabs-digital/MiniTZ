# Biella Engine — Intro Scene Brief

Status: APPROVED PRE-PRODUCTION CONTROL  
Version: 1.0  
Primary purpose: cinematic Biella Engine logo activation / loading / reveal sequence

## 1. Creative Objective

Create an AAA-quality mechanical activation sequence that expresses the meaning of **Biella** as the connecting mechanism between independent sources of force and unified execution.

The intro must not look like a generic AI logo reveal.

The audience should feel:
- mass;
- precision;
- tension;
- alignment;
- connection;
- ignition;
- controlled power;
- final mechanical confidence.

## 2. Core Story

The sequence is:

> **separate parts → movement → alignment → mechanical lock → sparks / energy fusion → activation → camera reveal → wordmark → tagline**

The final state must resolve exactly to the approved assembled Biella Engine symbol.

## 3. Primary Master Timing

Target master duration: **12 seconds**.

Timing may be adjusted during production for physical credibility, but the event order is locked.

### 0.00–1.50 — Separate parts

- dark industrial space;
- upper body, lower body, connecting rod and bearings exist as separate physical parts;
- components are clearly independent;
- camera establishes premium machined materials;
- no full logo silhouette yet;
- minimal purple spill, mostly dormant metal.

### 1.50–3.25 — Movement

- components begin moving on believable paths;
- heavy parts accelerate with mass, not weightless easing;
- rod translates and rotates toward the two connection points;
- micro-vibration and mechanical inertia may be used;
- camera moves with the assembly rather than floating randomly.

### 3.25–5.00 — Alignment

- upper and lower geometry settle into their final axes;
- bearings line up;
- the connecting rod enters the exact joint line;
- movement slows just before engagement;
- precision becomes the visual focus.

### 5.00–6.25 — Mechanical lock

- bearings seat;
- rod locks into both bodies;
- use a distinct physical contact event;
- a small compression / torque reaction may travel through the assembly;
- the final symbol becomes mechanically complete.

### 6.25–7.75 — Sparks / energy fusion

- industrial sparks or heat appear at the connection;
- restrained purple energy begins at the bearings and travels through the rod;
- orange exists as heat/spark energy only;
- no magical cloud or fantasy energy storm;
- energy should appear to follow real conductive / mechanical pathways.

### 7.75–9.00 — Activation

- purple activation reaches the upper and lower bodies;
- selected bevels / inserts emit or catch purple light;
- the system feels online;
- final symbol remains materially believable;
- bloom stays controlled.

### 9.00–10.25 — Camera reveal

- camera resolves to a strong front or 3/4 hero view;
- background falls away;
- symbol is fully readable;
- motion settles;
- any remaining particles decay.

### 10.25–11.25 — Wordmark

Reveal:

**BIELLA**  
**ENGINE**

Wordmark movement should be precise and minimal. Do not use glitch typography.

### 11.25–12.00 — Tagline

Reveal:

**CONNECTING INTELLIGENCE TO EXECUTION**

Hold long enough for legibility.

## 4. Mechanical Behavior

All motion must respect object hierarchy and real pivots.

Required:
- upper bearing rotation point;
- lower bearing rotation point;
- connecting-rod endpoints;
- correct parent/child relationships;
- no mesh sliding through another mesh;
- no fake lock created only with a dissolve;
- no impossible bending of rigid metal;
- no sudden teleportation.

The mechanical lock must work as real 3D motion.

## 5. Camera Language

Preferred:
- macro mechanical shots;
- controlled low-angle hero;
- shallow depth of field during assembly;
- stronger depth at final reveal;
- deliberate lens movement;
- no excessive handheld shake.

The sequence should feel closer to a premium AAA game studio ident / high-end automotive product film than a motion-graphics template.

## 6. Lighting Progression

Dormant state:
- dark gunmetal;
- narrow cool reflections;
- limited violet.

Alignment:
- stronger edge definition;
- contact points become readable.

Lock / ignition:
- orange sparks / heat;
- concentrated purple activation.

Final:
- polished corporate hero lighting;
- violet retained as identity;
- metal remains visible;
- black level remains rich, not crushed.

## 7. FX

Allowed:
- sparks;
- fine dust;
- subtle smoke;
- heat distortion;
- micro-debris;
- localized electrical arcs;
- purple particles;
- energy pulse through rod;
- short shock response on lock;
- controlled light sweep.

Avoid:
- giant explosion;
- fantasy lightning storm;
- holographic grid takeover;
- random code rain;
- generic AI particle sphere;
- excessive lens flares.

## 8. Audio Direction

Audio is not part of the first five deliverables, but the scene must be designed to support:

- heavy metallic movement;
- bearing slide / seat;
- mechanical lock;
- torque hit;
- ignition spark;
- low-frequency activation;
- restrained electrical energy;
- final clean sonic signature.

Visual timing should leave clear sync points for these events.

## 9. Derived Versions

The 12-second sequence is the master.

Future cutdowns may include:
- 8-second ident;
- 5-second splash;
- 3-second quick boot;
- static active-state loop;
- seamless loading loop.

Cutdowns must preserve the same final geometry.

## 10. Deliverable Intent

This scene must ultimately be reproducible from separated source objects and editable motion, not only from a flattened rendered video.

The scene is complete only when the requirements in `ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md` and `TECHNICAL_DELIVERY_SPEC.md` are satisfied.
