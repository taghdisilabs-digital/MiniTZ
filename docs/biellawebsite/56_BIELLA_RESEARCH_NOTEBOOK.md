# 56 — Research Notebook

## Purpose
A structured research workspace for collecting sources, observations, comparisons, citations, hypotheses, and unresolved questions without mixing research evidence with active product policy.

## Required detailed content
1. **Research mission** — objective, scope, assumptions, exclusions, and success criteria.
2. **Question decomposition** — primary question, subquestions, dependencies, and research sequence.
3. **Source collection** — URL/title, publisher, publication date, accessed date, source type, authority, freshness, and notes.
4. **Claim extraction** — important claims linked to exact source references and source passages.
5. **Citation manager** — citation IDs, quote/paraphrase notes, source word limits, and citation completeness.
6. **Comparison tables** — technologies, providers, APIs, architectures, prices, capabilities, constraints, and tradeoffs.
7. **Hypothesis board** — hypothesis, supporting evidence, opposing evidence, confidence, and validation status.
8. **Unresolved questions** — gaps, conflicting sources, stale information, and suggested next searches.
9. **Research timeline** — when evidence was found, revised, contradicted, or superseded.
10. **Findings synthesis** — separately label observed facts, inference, recommendation, and unknowns.
11. **Evidence bundle export** — selected sources, notes, comparisons, and citations as Markdown/JSON.
12. **Project-linking** — attach research findings to relevant Biella tasks, decisions, capabilities, or website claims.

## BIELLA VISUAL LOCK
Transparent background. Graphite/navy surfaces. Violet/cyan accents. Semantic status colors only. Dense technical research interface, compact typography, subtle glass, thin borders.

## Acceptance
Every synthesized finding remains traceable to exact evidence, and research notes do not silently become active architecture or policy.
