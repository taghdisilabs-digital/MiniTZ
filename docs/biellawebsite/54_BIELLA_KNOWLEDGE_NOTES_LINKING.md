# 54 — Knowledge Notes & Linking

## Purpose
A wiki-style Biella workspace for durable project notes, linked concepts, decisions, source provenance, and scope-aware knowledge navigation.

## Required detailed content
1. **Note tree and collections** — folders, pinned notes, tags, status, and scope badges for Engine, Project, Run, Historical Evidence, and Cache.
2. **Backlinks and outbound links** — exact target document/section anchors, unresolved-link warnings, and related-note suggestions.
3. **Knowledge graph** — notes, concepts, decisions, artifacts, tasks, runs, and sources as navigable linked entities.
4. **Provenance inspector** — source IDs, revisions, digests, timestamps, evidence class, originating task/run, and storage location.
5. **Scope classifier** — explicit placement into ENGINE MEMORY, PROJECT MEMORY, RUN MEMORY, HISTORICAL EVIDENCE, or CACHE.
6. **Contradiction and supersession panel** — conflicting claims, superseded notes, replacement links, and current accepted statement.
7. **Metadata editor** — tags, entities, applicability, confidence/status, owner/project, and last verification time.
8. **Promotion candidate panel** — observations that may later become versioned reusable knowledge, with evidence and destination scope.
9. **Search and filtering** — by concept, source, project, tag, status, confidence, date, or relation.
10. **Relationship inspector** — why two notes are linked and whether the relation is manual, source-derived, or inferred.

## Important behavior
Raw historical material is never instruction-active. Notes preserve logical scope even when identical physical content is deduplicated. Knowledge promotion is explicit, reviewable, versioned, and supersedable.

## BIELLA VISUAL LOCK
Transparent background. Deep graphite/navy surfaces. Biella violet dominant. Electric cyan secondary. Green, amber, and red only for semantic state. Thin precision borders, restrained bloom, subtle glass, compact professional typography.

## Acceptance
The tablet visibly contains the modules above and makes scope, provenance, contradiction, and knowledge status impossible to confuse.
